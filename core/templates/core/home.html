Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered.

What is Dhanya? +

Dhanya is an agricultural marketplace that connects producers with markets and consumers. Producers can list products while buyers can discover and order products according to their requirements.

Who can use Dhanya? +

Dhanya supports three main types of users: Producers, Markets and Consumers.

What can producers sell? +

Producers can list agricultural products such as fruits and vegetables, grains and pulses, dairy products, poultry and meat, and natural sweeteners.

Can markets place orders? +

Yes. Markets can browse available products and place orders based on the quantity they require.

Can consumers buy directly? +

Yes. Consumers can browse the Dhanya marketplace, select available products and place orders.
